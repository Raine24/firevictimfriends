FIELD ROLES

Emergency Response Cadre

Are you capable of maintaining extreme composure during crisis situations? Field deployment volunteers are our most physically-demanding and crucial asset during the immediate outbreak of localized disasters.

You will be trained to deploy directly to affected zones under the strict guidance of our professional response captains, securing perimeters, operating logistics pipelines, and setting up emergency shelter architecture.

  • Distribution of survival kits and heavy blankets.
  • Organizing temporary disaster triage camps.
  • Facilitating secure civilian evacuation routes.
PROACTIVE ROLES

Community Educators

If your passion is deeply rooted in proactive prevention rather than reactive deployment, the Education and Awareness branch requires massive, highly-motivated personnel.

Educators travel directly into vulnerable markets, densely populated slums, and schools to conduct life-saving fire safety workshops. You become the face of our prevention protocols, heavily impacting local safety awareness.

  • Presenting highly interactive safety demonstrations.
  • Distributing preventative literature and enforcing safety plans.
  • Training local leadership on proper crisis protocol implementation.
SPECIALIZED ROLES

Psychiatric & Therapy Assistants

Do you have a background in counseling, therapy, or childcare? The psychological devastation following a fire requires dedicated empathetic intervention that spans far beyond physical asset delivery.

These specialized cohorts operate directly within our trauma units, working 1-on-1 with victims or assisting registered clinicians in implementing child play therapies and managing widespread grief clinics.

  • Guiding victims through initial extreme shock trauma.
  • Co-facilitating local peer-support integration circles.
  • Running dedicated activities inside pediatric recovery camps.
