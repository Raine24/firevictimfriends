Our Journey of Impact:
Transforming Lives

From Tragedy to Action

Our journey began when founder Muhereza Augustine witnessed a devastating fire ravage a school dormitory, leaving hundreds without belongings. This trauma sparked a deep empathy and a vow to extend a helping hand to fire victims, and so FVF was born. We are committed to providing immediate relief, long-term support, and advocating for fire prevention and safety.

Our Mission

Fire victim friends (fvf) provides emotional support resources and relief to those affected by fires helping them rebuild their lives.

Our Vision

A world where fire victims receive compassionate support, empowering them to overcome tragedy and thrive.

Our Core Values

Compassion Empathy Support Integrity Community

Our Work: 5 Main Programs

1. Emergency Relief and Support

Objective: Provide immediate relief and support to fire victims in Uganda including food, shelter, clothing, financial and medical assistance.

Target Group: Fire victim in Uganda, affected in schools, homes, markets or vehicle fires.

  • Distribute emergency relief supplies eg food, blankets and clothing
  • Provide medical assistance and psychological support
  • Offer temporary shelter and accomodation program
LEARN MORE

2. Fire Prevention and Awareness

Objective: Raise awareness about fire prevention and safety measures in Uganda, particularly in schools, homes and markets.

Target Group: Communities, schools and market vendors in Uganda

  • Conduct fire safety workshops and training sessions.
  • Distribute safety materials such as brochures and posters.
  • Organise community events and campaigns.
LEARN MORE

3. Livelihood Support & Econ Dev

Objective: Support fire victims in rebuilding their livehoods and achieving economic empowerment

Target Group: Fire victims who lost their business or livelihoods.

  • Provide vocational training and business skills development
  • Offer microfinance and start up support for businesses
  • Provide tools and equipments for income generating activities.
LEARN MORE

4. Psychosocial Support & Trauma

Healing fire victims particularly children and vulnerable populations.

Target Group: Fire victims who have experienced trauma and distress

  • Conduct counseling and therapy sessions.
  • Organise support groups and peer-peer support networks
  • Provide play therapy and recreational activities for children.
LEARN MORE
5. Safety Infrastructure & FsF Grant

Fire safety infrastructure and equipment & FsF Grant Funding

Objective: Improve fire safety infrastructure and equipment in schools homes and markets in Uganda.

  • Install fire alarms and suppression systems
  • Provide fire extinguishers and other fire fighting equipments
  • Conduct fire safety inspection and risks assessments.
